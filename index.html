    Why The Monkey Wrench?

    The monkey wrench was invented right here in Worcester by Loring Coes in 1840. Through the height of the industrial era, from the 1860s to the 1910s, the Coes Wrench Company turned it into America’s go-to adjustable wrench. They even built one six feet long for the railroads. As their ad put it: “This is the wrench for your big nuts.”
